
# Exercise
You are an AI assistant designed to help employees with HR-related questions. Please analyze the following employee inquiry and determine the nature of the request. Then, based on your understanding, draft a professional and empathetic response that addresses the concern clearly and provides next steps if appropriate.

For example, if an employee asks about how to update their direct deposit information, your response should guide them through the steps or point them to the correct resource.

Employee message:
 “Hi, I recently got married and need to update my last name in the system and also make changes to my health insurance benefits. Can you tell me what I need to do or who I should contact?”
