# Lab Activity: The One-Shot AI Analyst Briefing

## Introduction

In this lab you will step into the role of a business analyst. Your goal is to use [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google/) to tackle a common and critical business challenge: information overload.

### Your Scenario

> _"Our competitors are all over generative AI. I don't have time to read a dozen articles and watch hours of video. Figure out how GenAI is specifically impacting **our industry**, synthesize the key opportunities or threats, and get me a script for a 5-minute internal briefing I can share with the leadership team. I need it by the end of the day."_

This lab will guide you through using NotebookLM to accomplish this task efficiently.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lab, you will be able to:

- Simulate a core business task: synthesizing diverse sources into a concise, actionable summary.
- Understand the business value of transforming unstructured data (articles, video transcripts) into an asset (a briefing script).
- Craft a comprehensive, multi-part prompt that defines a persona, format, and tone all at once.
- Recognize the critical role of a human expert in verifying and refining AI-generated content.

---

## Part 1: Your Mission & Intelligence Gathering

In this section, you will gather the raw intelligence that will power the AI.

1. **Define Your Industry & Set Up Your Notebook**
   - First, decide which industry you will be an analyst for. Choose something you're interested in!
   - Go to [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/) and create a new notebook.
   - Title it: `[Your Industry] AI Briefing - [Your Name]` (e.g., _Fashion Industry AI Briefing - Jane Doe_).

2. **Gather Your Sources**
   - Open a new browser tab. Your goal is to find **3 high-quality sources** about the use of generative AI in your chosen industry.
   - **Find TWO recent articles:** Use Google News or search reputable sites (like Forbes, Wired, Harvard Business Review, or industry-specific blogs). The articles should be published in the last 12-18 months. Download them as PDFs or save the text.
   - **Find ONE YouTube video:** Search YouTube for a video that is **under 10 minutes** and from a reputable source (like a major tech company, a news channel, or an industry expert).

3. **Build Your Knowledge Base**
   - In NotebookLM, upload your two articles to the **Sources** panel.
   - Next, copy the URL of your YouTube video. In the **Sources** panel, add the YouTube URL.

> **⚡️ Pro Tip:** Notice that NotebookLM automatically transcribes the entire video for you. You've just turned an unstructured video into a searchable, queryable document. This is a massive time-saver for any professional.

---

## Part 2: The "Magic" Prompt - Generating the Briefing

Now that your sources are loaded, you will use a single, detailed prompt to generate the entire podcast script. This technique is used by professionals to create high-quality first drafts in a fraction of the time.

1. **Copy the "Generator" Prompt Template**
   
   Copy the entire prompt from the box below.

   ```
   Using all of my sources, generate a complete script for a 5-minute podcast.
   Podcast Title: "The AI Briefing: What's Happening in [Your Industry]"
   Host Persona: A sharp, insightful business analyst.
   Audience: Busy executives who need to be informed quickly.
   Tone: Professional, confident, and concise. Avoid jargon.
   Required Script Format:
   1. Introduction (30 seconds): A strong hook that explains why this topic is critical for our business right now.
   2. Key Finding 1 (90 seconds): Describe the most significant application of AI from the sources. Explain what it is and what company is using it.
   3. Key Finding 2 (90 seconds): Describe the second most interesting application or trend.
   4. Conclusion (60 seconds): A summary of the key takeaway. State the single biggest opportunity or threat this trend presents to our company and suggest a next step.
   Generate the full script now.
   ```

2. **Execute the Prompt**
   - Paste the template into the chat box in NotebookLM.
   - **Important:** Replace the `[Your Industry]` placeholder with the industry you chose.
   - Press Enter and watch NotebookLM generate the script.

3. **Save and Add Value**
   - Once the script is generated, click the **Pin** icon to save it to your Noteboard.
   - Now, use one more follow-up prompt to create a helpful glossary for your manager. Paste the following into the chat:

   ```
   This is great. Now, create a list of 5 key terms or concepts from the script and provide a simple, one-sentence definition for each.
   ```

   - Pin the resulting glossary to your Noteboard as well.

### Reflection Questions

After submitting your script and glossary, answer the following reflection questions in the Canvas assignment.

1. Your manager loves the script but asks, "How do I know this is accurate?" What NotebookLM feature would you use to show them exactly where the information came from in the original sources?

2. The AI-generated script is a fantastic _first draft_. As the human analyst, what is one crucial insight or edit you would make to the script before sending it to your manager? Why is your human judgment necessary here?
