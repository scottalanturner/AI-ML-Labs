# Lab 4: Experience the Three Types of Machine Learning with ChatGPT

## Lab Overview

In this lab, you'll use ChatGPT to experience how the three types of machine learning work: supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning. You'll see how different approaches to "teaching" an AI system produce different results.

**What you'll need:**

- Access to ChatGPT (free version is fine)
- A Google Doc or Word document to record your answers

**What you'll submit:** A document with your answers from all three parts, including which approach worked best for each task and why (1-2 sentences each).

## Part 1: Sort Movie Reviews Three Ways

You'll give ChatGPT the same 10 movie reviews and ask it to sort them three different ways.

### Prompt 1: Supervised Learning

**Step 1:** Open ChatGPT and start a NEW chat.

**Step 2:** Copy and paste this ENTIRE box into ChatGPT:

> I'm going to give you 10 movie reviews with their correct labels. Learn from these examples:
> 
> **POSITIVE:**
> 1. "This movie was absolutely incredible with stunning visuals and amazing acting."
> 3. "Best film I've seen all year, truly a masterpiece!"
> 5. "The story kept me engaged from start to finish."
> 7. "A delightful surprise that exceeded all my expectations!"
> 9. "Brilliant cinematography and a touching story about family."
> 
> **NEGATIVE:**
> 2. "I fell asleep halfway through this boring mess."
> 4. "Complete waste of time and money, terrible in every way."
> 6. "Poorly written dialogue and confusing plot ruined everything."
> 8. "The worst sequel ever made, completely unnecessary."
> 10. "Predictable plot with wooden acting throughout."
> 
> Now that you've learned from these labeled examples, explain how you would identify positive vs negative reviews.

**Step 3:** Record ChatGPT's response in your document under "Part 1 - Supervised"

### Prompt 2: Unsupervised Learning
