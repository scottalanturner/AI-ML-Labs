# Build a Knowledge-Based Chatbot

**Tool:** Botpress Cloud (free account)

## Overview

Students will create a chatbot that can answer questions based on real website content and documents they provide, experiencing how modern AI chatbots learn from knowledge bases.

## Setup

1. Go to botpress.com and create a free account
2. Create a new chatbot using their setup wizard
3. Name your chatbot something relevant to your chosen topic

## Your Task

### Step 1: Choose Your Content Domain

Pick ONE of the following:

- A company or organization you're interested in
- A hobby or interest area (cooking, sports, gaming, etc.)
- An academic topic or educational resource
- A local business or service

**Real-World Example: Local Landscaping Business**

Here's a practical example you could follow:

Choose a local landscaping business that doesn't have a chatbot on their website. This chatbot could actually be something you could sell to them! Your chatbot will help their customers with lawn care questions.

### Step 2: Add Knowledge Sources

Add at least 2-3 knowledge sources to your chatbot:

- **Website:** Add one relevant website URL (example: a company's About page, a Wikipedia article, or a blog post)
- **Documents:** Upload 1-2 PDFs you have or can download (product manuals, research papers, info sheets, etc.)

**For the Landscaping Example:**

- Add the landscaping company's website (their services page, about page, etc.)
- Find and upload PDFs from your local agricultural extension service about:
  - Lawn care and maintenance guides
  - Common grass types in your region
  - Fertilization schedules
  - Disease identification and treatment guides
  - Seasonal yard maintenance tips

These PDFs are usually free from university extension services (search "[your state] extension service lawn care PDF").

**Note:** The Botpress interface will guide you through adding these to your knowledge base. Look for options related to "Knowledge Base," "Sources," or "Documents."

### Step 3: Build and Train

- Let the chatbot process and learn from your sources
- This may take a few minutes depending on the content

### Step 4: Test Your Chatbot

Test your chatbot by asking it at least 5 different questions about your content:

- Start with basic questions about the information you provided
- Try asking for specific details from your documents
- Ask something that combines information from multiple sources
- Test what happens when you ask about something NOT in your knowledge base

**Example Questions for a Landscaping Chatbot:**

- "What type of grass grows best in shade?"
- "When should I fertilize my lawn?"
- "What are signs of grub damage?"
- "How often should I water in summer?"
- "What services do you offer?"

### Step 5: Document Your Work

Take a screenshot showing:

- Your chatbot responding to a question
- The response should clearly show information pulled from your knowledge base

## Deliverable

Submit a screenshot of your chatbot successfully answering a question based on your uploaded knowledge sources.

## What You're Learning

- How modern chatbots use knowledge bases instead of pre-programmed responses
- The difference between retrieval-based AI (finding information) and generative AI
- Why the quality of source materials matters for chatbot responses
- Real-world applications of knowledge management in AI systems
- How to create a valuable tool that businesses would actually pay for

## Tips

- Choose reliable, well-written sources - better input means better output
- Pick a focused topic rather than something too broad
- Public websites and non-copyrighted PDFs work best
- The chatbot can only answer based on what you give it - it won't know information you haven't provided
- Think like a business owner: What questions do customers ask repeatedly that a chatbot could handle?
